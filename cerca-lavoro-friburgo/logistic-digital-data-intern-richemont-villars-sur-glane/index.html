Reference code: JR132522

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

This internship will provide you with hands-on experience in logistics performance management, master data and digital transformation. You will be fully integrated in Richemont Internal Distribution Logistic Agility team to contribute to different projects and initiatives. Your key responsibilities will include:

DATA & ANALYTICS

  • Logistics Reporting Support: Assist in setting up and improving logistics dashboards, ensuring alignment with global KPIs and reporting guidelines.
  • Continuous Improvement: Participate in logistics optimization projects and develop new methodologies to enhance performance measurement.
  • Data Management Support: Use BI tools (Power BI, SAP, GCP, Looker) to analyze and visualize logistics performance data.

DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION

  • Process Analysis & Optimization: Map existing processes to identify inefficiencies, manual bottlenecks, and key opportunities for digitalization and automation.
  • Solution Design & Implementation: Support the design, development, and deployment of logistic digital tools.
  • Project Support: Contribute to ongoing digital transformation initiatives, supporting the implementation as well as helping with documentation.

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

You are currently pursuing a Master’s/Bachelor’s degree in Business, Engineering, or a related field, with a strong interest in logistics, performance management, and data analysis or digital transformation,

Proficiency in Excel, Power BI, SAP, SQL and capable of structuring and managing databases.

Proactive and autonomous, with a self-driven mindset and a can-do attitude.

Detail-oriented and rigorous, able to synthesize and communicate complex analyses effectively.

Strong analytical skills, with the ability to interpret data/processes and provide actionable recommendations.

Excellent interpersonal and teamwork skills, to collaborate with various internal stakeholders.

Fluent in French and English.

WHAT MAKES OUR GROUP DIFFERENT?

At Richemont, we embrace a culture of performance and continuous improvement. We foster diversity, collaboration, and innovation, providing an engaging and dynamic environment to tackle tomorrow’s logistics challenges.

YOUR JOURNEY WITH US

If your application is selected, we will contact you for an initial discussion. You will then have the opportunity to meet the team and gain insights into the role’s challenges and expectations.
