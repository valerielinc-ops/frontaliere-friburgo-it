Reference code: JR130200 The story of Cartier is founded on curiosity and passion. For more than 170 years we have embraced a bold, pioneering spirit that continues to inspire our teams across all Métiers from our boutiques to our workshops and corporate offices. Our 10,000+ colleagues of more than 105 nationalities are united by a shared independent spirit and commitment to excellence, striving to continuously enrich our Maison’s heritage by pushing the boundaries of creativity and innovation.

End of studies internship with school agreement

Duration: 6 months minimum

Location: 1752 Villars-sur-Glâne, Switzerland

Context

The objective of this internship is to enrich your academic background with a stimulating professional experience in an international environment.

How will you make an impact?

In our Supply Chain department in charge of the distribution of finished goods, you will be part of a highly established team and you will contribute to the following missions:

Carry out business analysis for the definition and implementation of the supply strategy to ensure optimum stock level, challenge forecast and loops with all stakeholders,

Collaborate with markets, marketing and external supplier on their problematics,

Create reporting and maintain existing reports, particularly under Power BI or Anaplan

Participate in continuous improvement projects related to the department activity

Your profile

You are in your last year master’s degree in engineering with a Supply Chain focus.

You have advanced skills in Excel and/or experience with Power BI.

You have robust analytical skills, and you easily analyze and work with Data and Information Systems.

You are organized, autonomous and reliable.

You have a very good command of French and English.

How do we keep you smiling?

You will be a part of a dynamic and international work environment, where we always go the extra mile to uphold outstanding service for our clients

Your journey with us

If your application is selected, we will reach out to you ASAP for an informal introductory call. The next step will be an introductory call with one of our Data Scientists, followed by a technical interview and a business case.
